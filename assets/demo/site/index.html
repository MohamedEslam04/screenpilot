Install in one step

تثبيت بخطوة واحدة على أي نظام

  1. Download the installer and double-click it.
  2. Click Install. No admin password, nothing else to install.
  3. Restart your AI app. ScreenPilot is already there.
⬇ ScreenPilot-windows-setup.exe
  1. Unzip the download for your Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel).
  2. Double-click Install ScreenPilot.command.
  3. Restart your AI app and allow Screen Recording when asked.
⬇ ScreenPilot-macos-arm64.zip
  1. Extract the download.
  2. Double-click install.sh (or run ./install.sh).
  3. Restart your AI app. Works on KDE, GNOME, Mint, Xfce and more.
⬇ ScreenPilot-linux.tar.gz

How it works

إزاي بيشتغل

1
Record

The built-in recorder captures your screens, the pointer and every click. No OBS.

2
Look

Your AI looks at the footage and finds the moments that matter.

3
Edit

It cuts idle time, zooms on clicks and adds captions in any language.

4
Render

A polished MP4, ready to share. Everything stays on your computer.
