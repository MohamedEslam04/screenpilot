Documentation

Install ScreenPilot, ask your AI app for a video, and learn every tool, command and setting.

Start

  • Getting startedWhat ScreenPilot does, how a video gets made in four steps, and your first video in three asks to your AI app.
  • Install ScreenPilotInstall ScreenPilot on Windows, macOS or Linux, add it to your AI apps, uninstall it, and fix common problems.

Use it

  • Command lineEvery screenpilot command and its useful flags, grouped by job, with the full record, edit and render workflow in one block.
  • MCP toolsThe tools ScreenPilot gives an AI app, grouped by job: recording, footage, storyboard, render, desktop control and diagnostics.
  • Storyboard referenceThe storyboard.json format: segments, holds, zooms, captions, highlights, narration, intro and outro, music, theme and output, with a full example.
  • Let the agent driveThe agent performs the demo itself on your real desktop: write a take.json, rehearse it, perform it while recording, then edit and render.

Reference

  • Safety and privacyWhat the agent can touch when it drives your desktop, how to stop it, how to limit it, what is logged, and what leaves your computer.
  • PlatformsWhat ScreenPilot can do on each desktop: recording, driving, window control and click logging, plus requirements, per-OS notes and limits.
  • FAQ and troubleshootingShort answers to common questions about ScreenPilot, plus fixes for the problems people hit most: permissions, dialogs, freezes and missing zooms.
