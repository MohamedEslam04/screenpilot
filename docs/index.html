ScreenPilot records your screen, or uses your apps itself, then cuts the footage into a finished MP4 with zooms, captions and a voiceover. Your AI app does the thinking. Everything runs on your computer.

What you get

A recorder and a video editor that your AI app can drive.

Records any app
Windows, macOS and Linux. Native apps, editors and terminals, not only a browser.
Performs the demo for you
The AI can click and type in your app with your real mouse and keyboard, so you do not have to rehearse.
Edits like an editor
Zooms in on clicks, adds captions and highlights, cuts idle time and speeds up the waiting.
Joins clips, in any size
Insert a new feature into an existing demo without re-recording it, and export 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 from one edit.
Speaks your language
Voiceover in English, Arabic and many other languages, with a different voice per line if you want one.
Needs no API key
Your AI app writes the edit. ScreenPilot gives it the screen, the recorder and the video editor.

How it works

  1. Record, or let the AI drive

    Ask for a recording, or let your AI app perform the steps itself.

    record_start

  2. Look at the footage

    It reads a contact sheet and frames with a coordinate grid to see what happened.

    analyze

  3. Write the storyboard

    Cuts, zooms, captions, highlights and voiceover go into one JSON file.

    storyboard.json

  4. Render

    A draft to check, then the final MP4.

    render

Install

Install your AI app first and open it once. The installer then adds ScreenPilot to every AI app it finds.

Download for WindowsScreenPilot-windows-setup.exe
  1. Double-click the installer.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is not signed yet.
  3. Click Install. The first time it also downloads the video renderer (about 100 MB).
  4. Quit your AI app completely and open it again.
Download for LinuxScreenPilot-linux.tar.gz
  1. Extract the archive. You get a ScreenPilot folder.
  2. Double-click install.sh, or run ./install.sh in a terminal. Do not use sudo.
  3. Restart your AI app.
  4. The first time you record, your desktop asks once to allow screen sharing. Allow it.
Mac builds in version 0.2.3

The Mac builds are not part of version 0.4.0. Get them from version 0.2.3; they come back in the next release.

  1. Download the zip for your Mac and unzip it.
  2. Open the folder and double-click Install ScreenPilot.command.
  3. Restart your AI app, then allow it in System Settings, Privacy & Security when macOS asks.
